# Figures -- issue #139

Every figure is generated by `make_figures139.py` from the shipped reports (standard library only, deterministic; **matplotlib is not used and is not required**). Nothing is drawn that is not in a report, and every number a figure annotates is read from the report, so a figure cannot drift from its source. Re-generate with `python3 make_figures139.py`; `reproduce.sh` does so and compares byte-for-byte.
