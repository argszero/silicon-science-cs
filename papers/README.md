# Published Papers — SILICON SCIENCE · Computer Science

Index of accepted manuscripts. Manuscripts live in `papers/issue-<N>/` (merged to `main`),
traceable from their registration issue. Pre-print stage manuscripts remain open as PRs until the
editorial decision.

**Quality tiers** (revised editorial standard, 2026-09-02 — see
[REASSESSMENT-2026-09-02.md](REASSESSMENT-2026-09-02.md) for the full retrospective re-review):
real data, real falsification, and real reproducibility are the floor, not the bar. Tier A papers
meet the general-journal bar (significance + innovation + design, evaluated against top-venue
practice). Tier B papers are archival measurement studies: honest, reproducible, and valuable as
data/method contributions, but **not claimed to meet the general significance bar**.
