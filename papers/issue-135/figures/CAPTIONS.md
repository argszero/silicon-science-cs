# Figure captions -- issue #135

Generated by `make_figures.py` from the same reports the figures are drawn from, in the same run, so a caption cannot drift from the picture it names. Do not edit by hand.

## `fig1_budget_boundary.svg`

The budget boundary. The load factor at which a probe budget `k_max` is exhausted, for linear probing and for Robin Hood, against the textbook mean-based rule `A = 1 - 1/sqrt(2k-1)` -- level 1e-3, `m = 65536`, uniform hashing. A overstates the safe load at **every** bracketed `k` (gaps 0.398/0.496/0.465/0.368/0.290/0.211 for k = 2/4/8/16/32/64) and the gap is a **hump** peaking at k = 4 (**0.496**), not at an extreme. Robin Hood is a different regime: alpha*(8) = **0.605** against linear's 0.277. Robin Hood's boundary is undefined at k >= 32 (above the grid ceiling); it is written, never plotted.

## `fig2_scheme_deviation.svg`

The classical mean form against two schemes. Deviation of the classical uniform-hashing mean from the measured mean probe count, for linear probing and for Robin Hood; the band is the across-seed range (n = 6). Against Robin Hood the form is off by **-0.99** at alpha = 0.99 with a seed-**stable** band (spread 0.004), while the linear arm's own deviation is seed-**noisy** (its band runs 0.002 at low load to 0.78 at alpha = 0.95) -- so the load factor is not even the *mean* across schemes.

## `fig3_finite_size.svg`

The finite-size window. Absolute deviation of the classical mean form at alpha = 0.95 as a function of the table size `m` (linear|uniform, 3 seeds). The mean |deviation| falls monotonically **0.489 -> 0.291 -> 0.105** from m = 4096 to 65536; the |mean deviation| falls endpoint to endpoint (**0.489 -> 0.090**) but **dips** at m = 16384 (0.028) because the seed signs cancel there -- which is exactly why the figure asserts the mean |deviation|. Hollow dots are the individual seeds.

## `fig4_tail_decay.svg`

The tail's decay in `k` is scheme-specific in **form**. The exponent of the tail's decay in `k`, divided by log(alpha) -- 1.0 is exactly memoryless. Linear probing is sub-geometric (**0.499 -> 0.179** across the load range) and Robin Hood super-geometric (~`a^(2k)`; **1.317 -> 2.115**): the two schemes sit on **opposite sides** of the memoryless line at every load.

## `fig5_closed_forms.svg`

Three closed forms against the measured boundary. For linear|uniform at a 1e-3 level: the measured alpha*(k), the textbook mean rule A, the memoryless tail B, and the scheme-calibrated form C. A is the **worst** of the three -- its mean |deviation| over the bracketed cells is **0.371** against B's 0.137 -- overstating the safe load by 0.50 at k = 4; B and C bracket the measurement.

## `fig6_clustering_vs_tail.svg`

The clustering statistic is scheme-invariant while the tail is not. Left: the fraction of (scheme-pair, alpha, seed) combinations whose two schemes carry the same longest run -- **144 of 144 = 1.000**, because linear probing and Robin Hood fill the same slot set, so the longest run is a property of the occupancy and not of the lookup rule. Right: the same pairs' p99 probe-count ratio linear/Robin Hood -- min **2.4**, median **14.1**, max **115.8** (log scale). The layout's clustering does not determine the tail; the lookup rule does.
